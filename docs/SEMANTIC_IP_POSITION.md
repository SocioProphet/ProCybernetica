# IP position — semantic coordinate algebra
### Jurisdictional analysis of the third-party licence claims, and the clean-room posture

Status: **position of record. Not legal advice** — counsel sign-off and a real
freedom-to-operate search are still required (§12) and neither has happened.

Published deliberately, for the reason in §10: the record of what was examined and
deliberately not taken is the evidence of independent creation.
---

## 1. Executive position

**Lévy's enforceable perimeter is the dictionary text, the translations, and the
grammar prose. It is not the algebra.** The algebra — six primitives, ternary
non-commutative multiplication over substance/attribute/mode, the layer system, the
nine syntagmatic roles, paradigms-as-symmetry-groups — is an unprotectable *system*
in every jurisdiction that matters.

That split is convenient, because **the parts we cannot have are the parts we do not
want.** The dictionary is IEML's liability (one author, ~3000 hand-assigned meanings,
humanities-only by design, FR/EN only, unevaluated), not its asset.

**The "it's derived from Kabbalah" argument should not be used.** It is both factually
shaky and legally unnecessary — see §2.

---

## 2. Why the "derived from scripture" argument fails, and what replaces it

### 2.1 It is legally the wrong test
Copyright in a work built on public-domain sources still subsists in whatever
**original expression the author added**. Drawing on Kabbalah, Peirce, or Spinoza does
not void rights in the dictionary text. Derivative-work reasoning gets us nothing:
Lévy would simply hold copyright in his contribution, which is exactly the dictionary
we want to avoid anyway.

### 2.2 It is factually weak
IEML is not meaningfully Kabbalah-derived:
- The 6 primitives are **his own admitted convention** — *"We could have chosen other
  primitives and arrived at other concepts… Not only are languages conventional, but
  they cannot not be conventional."*
- substance / attribute / mode is **Spinoza**, credited in the Grammar.
- sign / being / thing is **Peirce's** triad.
- The 9 syntagmatic roles are **Tesnière's actants** + standard case grammar.

The Kabbalistic resonance is in *our* Part I source material, not in IEML. Building a
legal position on a factual claim that will not survive scrutiny is a bad trade when a
stronger argument is free.

### 2.3 The argument that actually works
**A formal language and its algebra are a system. Systems are not copyrightable.**

## 3. Layer A — the algebra is unprotectable (copyright)

| Jurisdiction | Authority | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| **EU** | **CJEU C-406/10, *SAS Institute v World Programming*** (2 May 2012) | "Neither the functionality of a computer program **nor the programming language** and the format of data files… constitute a form of expression of that program" — **not protected by copyright**. Protecting functionality would monopolise ideas. |
| **US** | **17 U.S.C. §102(b)** | No protection for "any idea, procedure, process, **system**, method of operation, concept, principle, or discovery." |
| **US** | ***Baker v. Selden*** (1879) | The system described in a book is not protected by the copyright in the book. |
| **US** | ***Google v. Oracle*** (2021) | Reimplementing an interface for interoperability — fair use even assuming copyrightability. |
| **Intl** | **TRIPS Art 9(2)**, Berne | Expression only; not ideas, procedures, methods of operation, or mathematical concepts. |
| **Conlangs** | ***Paramount v. Axanar*** (C.D. Cal. 2016) | Court indicated the Klingon language "may not be individually original and copyright protectable"; Paramount **dropped** the language claim. Language Creation Society amicus is the canonical statement. |
| **Conlangs** | **Loglan → Lojban** (1992) | The live precedent for exactly this manoeuvre: a clean reconstruction after a dispute, which survived. (That case was *trademark*; the Loglan mark was cancelled as generic.) |

**⇒ The ring, the ternary multiplication, the three morphological roles, the layers,
the nine syntagmatic roles, and the paradigm-as-symmetry-group construct are all
free.** The CC BY-NC-ND licence does not even reach them: a CC licence only restricts
uses that *require permission*, and using an unprotected system requires none.

## 4. Layer B — what *is* protectable

| Asset | Basis | Strength |

## 5. Layer C — the patent claim, checked

INTLEKT/Lévy publicly state IEML is *"patented (provisional: **US 63/124,924**)"*,
assigned to INTLEKT Metadata Inc.

**What a provisional is:** a priority placeholder. It confers **no enforceable rights
whatsoever**, is **never examined**, and **lapses after 12 months** unless converted
to a non-provisional or PCT. The `63/xxx,xxx` series dates it to ~**December 2020** →
conversion deadline ~**December 2021** → publication of any conversion ~**mid-2022**.

**Searches run 2026-08-01 against Google Patents (indexes US, CA, EP publications):**

| Query | Results |
|---|---|
| `"Information Economy MetaLanguage"` | **0** |
| inventor `Pierre Levy` + `semantic metalanguage` | **0** |
| assignee `INTLEKT` / `INTLEKT Metadata` | **nothing** |

**⇒ Strong indication the provisional lapsed and no enforceable patent exists.**

Not conclusive — index gaps and non-publication requests are possible — so counsel
must run a proper freedom-to-operate search before public release. But *"IEML is
patented"* as currently stated is, on the public record, **marketing rather than an
enforceable right**.

**And if it had been converted**, the claim would face:
- **US:** *Alice* §101 — a method of representing meaning via an algebra is squarely
  an abstract idea.
- **EU:** **EPC Art 52(2)(a)** — mathematical methods *as such* are excluded from
  patentability.

Low probability of a granted, enforceable claim over the algebra.

## 6. Layer D — the two rights that survive regardless

**Trademark.** "IEML" and "INTLEKT" are likely marks of INTLEKT Metadata Inc. Do not
name the system IEML; do not imply endorsement or affiliation. Nominative reference
("differs from IEML in that…") is permitted. Costless to comply with.

**Moral rights.** Lévy is French; France's *droit à la paternité* is **inalienable and
perpetual** — attribution cannot be contracted away for a **protected work**. But
moral rights attach only to protected works; they do not attach to an unprotectable
system. **If we use none of his expression, no attribution is legally owed in any
jurisdiction.**

## 7. Governing law

INTLEKT is Canadian; Lévy is French-Canadian; the works were published from Canada
(research funded 2002–2016 via the Canada Research Chair in Collective Intelligence,
U. Ottawa). But **infringement is territorial** — what governs is where we *copy,
host, and distribute*. For this estate that means Canada + US + EU simultaneously.

- **EU** is simultaneously the *worst* jurisdiction (database right) and the *best*
  (*SAS v WPL* eliminates any claim over the language itself).
- **Canada** and the **US** have no database right at all.

**Net: the split is clean in every jurisdiction — take the algebra, leave the lexicon.**

## 8. Doing better — seven improvements

Strategic reframe: **inheriting his dictionary would mean inheriting his blind spots
to avoid work we should do anyway.**

### 8.1 Derive the primitives from the sources, not from his selection
He concedes the six are conventional. So derive ours from the public-domain sources
the estate is **already aligned to**:
- **Peirce's Firstness / Secondness / Thirdness** (1890s, PD) — already KKO's spine;
  this is what `S:/B:/T:` reaches for.
- **Aristotelian potentiality / actuality** (PD) — the settled matter/form core
  (*chomer/tzurah*) already bound to `kko:Matter` / `kko:Forms`; this is `U:/A:`.
- A **neutral/zero element** is a mathematical necessity, not anyone's invention.

⇒ A structurally equivalent primitive set that is **natively KKO-aligned** — which
IEML is not — and clean-room *by construction*, with provenance in Peirce and
Aristotle rather than Lévy.

### 8.2 Ground every address
IEML gives structure with no way to resolve to a world entity. `SemanticAddress`
carries both faces: intensional (algebraic decomposition) **+** extensional
(KKO IRI → Wikidata QID). The single largest capability gap, and we already own the
other half.

### 8.3 Carry the warrant
IEML has inference-type and logical-mood inflections but **no calibration, no evidence
pointer, no provenance chain, no revocation, no validity interval**. Add:
`{ inference, mood, confidence, evidenceRef, validFrom, validUntil, revocationRef }`.
A semantic address that carries its own warrant is something IEML structurally cannot
express — and it is the entire point of this estate.

### 8.4 Pluralistic, versioned lexicons
Bind **many** lexicons to **one** algebra; make the lexicon a parameter, not a canon.
Kills the coverage problem (exact sciences, health, capital markets), kills the
bus-factor problem, and dissolves the ND problem by construction.

### 8.5 Derive paradigms instead of hand-authoring them
KBpedia's **~30 disjoint typologies are already symmetry groups** — precisely the
structure IEML hand-tables. Generate the paradigm matrices from them: machine-derived,
checkable, extensible, and it recycles work already done.

### 8.6 Model drift
IEML addresses are static; meaning is not. Apply vendor-freshness-plane thinking — an
address has a validity interval and the graph can reason about its staleness. This is
Gevurah **G3** in the Da'at 5G set.

### 8.7 Evaluate from day one
The KKO board's lesson: elegant grounding fired on 345/350 rows and moved nothing
(+0.3pp, noise). Gate on **abstraction-level mismatch rate**, n ≥ 30 per cell, above
the 7B ceiling, with the layer bar proven to **reject** as well as pass.

## 9. Clean-room discipline

This doubles as the warrant — same pattern as the design register.

**Provenance register** — one row per design element: `element · public-domain source ·
citation · "not taken from IEML" note`. Cheap to maintain, and it is the evidence.

**Hard rules**
- Never copy a dictionary entry, definition, translation, or USL string.
- Never build a mapping table keyed on his USLs (arguably a derivative of the
  Dictionary and an extraction under the EU database right).
- Never redistribute the Grammar or Dictionary PDFs.
- Never link the GPL-3.0 `IEMLdev/ieml` code into any estate service.
- Never vendor the unlicensed repos (`ieml-language`, `ieml-dictionary`,
  `ieml-reasoner`) — no licence means all rights reserved.

**Separation of duties.** Whoever has read the third party's dictionary materials
writes only *functional specifications of unprotected elements* (the algebra, the role
inventory). Implementation proceeds from that spec plus the public-domain sources.

**Naming.** No third-party mark is used to name any artifact of ours — no module,
class, schema title, or filename. Referring to their system in prose is a different
act and is permitted (§10).

**Mechanized, not promised.** `procyber/semantic/check_cleanroom.py` runs in CI over
every registered framework artifact and fails the build on: transcribed dictionary
markers (zero tolerance), coordinate tokens past a citation threshold, and a
third-party mark used to name one of our artifacts. Its manifest has a completeness
test, so a new module or doc added without registration fails rather than passing
unscanned.

An earlier version of that guard failed on any *mention* of the third party. That
tested the wrong invariant and was replaced. A copying claim requires access **and**
substantial similarity of protected expression; a name-grep addresses neither. It
would have passed a file that transcribed the dictionary without naming it, and
failed a single truthful sentence of comparison.

## 10. Attribution posture

**Legally owed: none**, provided no protected expression is used (§6).

**Publicly cited: Peirce, Spinoza, Aristotle, Tesnière** — the actual provenance of
every generating element, registered row by row in `SEMANTIC_COORDINATE_ALGEBRA.md`.

**The prior-art comparison is published, not hidden** (`SEMANTIC_PRIOR_ART_COMPARISON.md`).
Naming a third party in truthful comparison is nominative reference and is lawful.
The earlier posture here — keep it internal, because publishing establishes *access* —
weighed one element of a copying claim while ignoring the other. Access is harmless
where no protected expression was taken, and a documented record of what was examined
and deliberately declined is the ordinary way independent creation is evidenced. The
risk that remains is copied expression and trademark use in naming, and both are
controlled mechanically (§9) rather than by silence.
