# Research Runway: AI, Quantum Learning, and Intelligence Governance

## Purpose

This document records the long-horizon research runway for the Cybernetic Governance Fabric.

It is deliberately staged. Not every foundation is a week-one primitive. The immediate Tier 1 target is a schema-validated governance MVP. The runway names the deeper apparatus that makes the fabric frontier-grade over a five-year horizon.

## Staging correction

Two staging commitments are now canonical:

1. **Tier 0 precedes schemas.** Constitutional invariants come before Tier 1 schemas.
2. **CP-SNARK evidence receipts move to late Tier 2.** They do not require completion of all Tier 3 mathematics; they require implementation discipline, commitment schemes, receipt schemas, and a suitable proving stack.

## Synthesis

The moat is not one technique. It is the intersection of four structures:

1. compositional structure;
2. counterfactual structure;
3. geometric structure;
4. cryptographic/complexity-theoretic structure.

A governance fabric that is simultaneously compositional, counterfactual, geometric, and cryptographic is structurally stronger than a collection of evals and policy documents.

## Non-claim boundary

This runway is not an implementation claim. It names a staged research program and the conditions under which each area should be promoted into active development.
