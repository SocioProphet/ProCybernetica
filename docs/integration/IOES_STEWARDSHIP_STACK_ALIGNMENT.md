# IOES Stewardship Stack Alignment

## Purpose

This document aligns the emerging IOES stewardship doctrine with the SocioProphet control, policy, execution, delivery, and learning repositories.

IOES means Identity, Ontogenesis, Ecology, and Stewardship.

Identity asks what persists.

Ontogenesis asks what develops.

Ecology asks what interconnects and sustains.

Stewardship asks what is entrusted and who is responsible for its continuity.

The goal is to prevent the human-protection platform from collapsing into a profile system, task automation system, social-credit system, or abstract knowledge graph. The intended object is a stewarded developmental entity embedded in an ecological network across time.

## Repository roles

### ProCybernetica

ProCybernetica is the doctrine-as-code and cybernetic-control law surface.

It should define the control vocabulary, promotion law, conformance posture, effective-authority boundaries, and assurance packaging for IOES-aware systems.

ProCybernetica should not implement downstream runtime adapters. It should specify the lawful control conditions that downstream systems must satisfy.

IOES responsibility:

- define IOES control-node doctrine;
- define stewardship and ontogenesis as cybernetic lifecycle obligations;
- define effective-authority hardening for human-impacting agents;
- define conformance lanes for IOES claims, actions, and promotions;
- preserve the distinction between doctrine, executable spec, and production runtime.

### Policy Fabric

Policy Fabric is the policy-as-code veto and decision surface.

It should carry machine-readable policies that prevent unsafe identity fusion, unconsented projection, unauthorized stewardship transfer, developmental-state mutation, ecological harm externalization, and agent overreach.

IOES responsibility:

- encode non-negotiable vetoes for dignity, consent, stewardship, provenance, succession, and protected values;
- require evidence and review for stewardship assignment, transfer, and revocation;
- require policy snapshots for learning, projection, execution, and delivery flows;
- preserve fail-closed semantics for human-impacting actions;
- provide exception procedures without allowing exception goblinry to eat the constitution.

### AgentPlane

AgentPlane is the execution control plane and evidence surface.

It should run governed bundles and produce tamper-evident Validation, Placement, Run, Replay, Promotion, Reversal, Session, and supply-chain evidence artifacts.

IOES responsibility:

- execute IOES-impacting bundles only after policy and authority checks;
- emit evidence for any agent action that may affect identity, stewardship, developmental state, projection, curriculum, or delivery status;
- support replayable investigation of agent decisions;
- bind execution artifacts to Regis, Human Digital Twin, HolographMe, Policy Fabric, and ProCybernetica conformance expectations;
- refuse silent mutation of human-impacting graph state.

### Delivery Excellence

Delivery Excellence is the operational outcome and scorecard surface.

It should translate stewardship and development doctrine into measurable operating cadence, roles, KPI/OKR definitions, delivery evidence, and reusable work packaging.

IOES responsibility:

- define stewardship-aware delivery metrics;
- distinguish output metrics from human-development metrics;
- measure succession risk, stewardship debt, learning continuity, delivery reliability, and evidence completeness;
- prevent delivery scorecards from becoming surveillance or coercive productivity dashboards;
- define review cadences for human-agent teams and customer proof.

### Alexandrian Academy

Alexandrian Academy is the curriculum, canonization, and lifelong-learning surface.

It already treats evidence, policy, provenance, standards, and promotion as inseparable for learning objects. It is the natural home for lifelong learning, mentorship, canon/sandbox promotion, and knowledge-stewardship flows.

IOES responsibility:

- model learning objects as stewarded artifacts;
- model learners as developmental entities without reducing them to scores;
- attach curriculum claims to verifiable evidence anchors;
- preserve promotion gates from sandbox to canon;
- define mentor, apprentice, successor, and knowledge-lineage relationships;
- integrate with Human Digital Twin without turning the twin into an educational surveillance record.

## Cross-stack flow

The intended IOES flow is:

1. A person, agent, repository, learning object, project, community, or ecosystem is represented as a Living Entity.
2. Identity Is Prime protects prime-topic boundaries and prevents unsafe fusion.
3. Regis materializes entity, evidence, stewardship, ecological, and developmental graph state.
4. Gaia supplies world-model and ecological dependency context.
5. Human Digital Twin stores consented continuity claims and export readiness state.
6. HolographMe emits consent-scoped projections.
7. Alexandrian Academy supplies evidence-grounded learning, canonization, mentorship, and transmission structures.
8. Policy Fabric decides which actions, projections, promotions, and transfers are allowed.
9. AgentPlane executes allowed bundles and emits replayable evidence.
10. Delivery Excellence measures outcomes without collapsing people into productivity objects.
11. ProCybernetica defines the lawful control-node doctrine and conformance posture across the whole system.

## Required IOES invariants

A person is not their account, twin, projection, score, role, credential, or agent summary.

A digital twin is a bounded consented projection, not a human subject.

Stewardship does not imply ownership.

Mentorship does not imply control.

Learning records do not imply surveillance rights.

Delivery metrics do not imply human worth.

Agent action does not imply human approval unless explicitly authorized.

Ecological dependency must not be hidden merely because it is inconvenient to model.

Developmental state must not be changed without authority, evidence, and review.

Every human-impacting action must be policy-bound, evidence-backed, replayable, and revocable where revocation is meaningful.

## Minimum shared artifact families

The stack should converge on these artifact families.

IdentityArtifact records continuity and prime-topic boundaries.

OntogenesisState records developmental phase, history, trajectory, and review posture.

GaiaDependencyRecord records ecological and system dependencies.

StewardshipEdge records responsibility without ownership.

KeeperLog records who accepted stewardship, when, under what scope, and with what review duties.

SuccessionRule records how stewardship transfers under normal, emergency, or abandonment conditions.

ConsentReceipt records scoped, revocable authorization.

PolicyDecision records allow, deny, repair, review, and exception decisions.

ExecutionEvidence records validation, placement, run, replay, promotion, reversal, and supply-chain facts.

LearningCanonChangeSet records movement from draft or sandbox knowledge to reviewed canon.

DeliveryOutcomeRecord records outcome evidence without reducing humans to productivity scores.

## Control-node implications

Under ProCybernetica, every meaningful component is a lawful control node.

For IOES, that means each control node must declare:

- what it can observe;
- what it can infer;
- what it can propose;
- what it can mutate;
- what it can export;
- what policy gates constrain it;
- what evidence it emits;
- how it is replayed;
- how it is revoked or repaired;
- what human-impacting consequences it may trigger.

Any component that cannot answer these questions is not ready for human-impacting authority.

## First implementation tranche

The first executable tranche should be deliberately narrow.

Scenario: keep a learning artifact.

A user or community creates a learning artifact in Alexandrian Academy.

The artifact cites evidence anchors.

A keeper accepts stewardship.

A mentor/apprentice or successor relation is recorded.

Policy Fabric checks whether the artifact can be promoted, projected, assigned, or delivered.

AgentPlane executes validation and emits replayable evidence.

Regis materializes the identity, evidence, stewardship, consent, and developmental graph edges.

Human Digital Twin records only consented learner-continuity claims.

HolographMe emits a projection that says what is known, what is omitted, what is consented, what is provisional, and what stewardship duties exist.

Delivery Excellence records outcome evidence and review cadence without converting the learner into a productivity object.

ProCybernetica validates that the whole loop satisfies lawful control-node requirements.

## Non-goals

This document does not implement the IOES ontology, schemas, validators, or runtime adapters.

It does not grant authority to agents.

It does not define production readiness.

It does not replace existing repository-specific contracts.

It aligns the stack so the next tranches can become schemas, fixtures, validators, and execution evidence without inventing conflicting local doctrine.
